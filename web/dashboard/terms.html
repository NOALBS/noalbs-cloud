What NOALBS Cloud is

NOALBS Cloud watches the stats of the stream server your encoder pushes to and switches your OBS between scenes (LIVE, LOW, BRB) based on the thresholds you set. It also offers a dashboard to control OBS remotely, stream history, a chat bot for Twitch, Kick and YouTube, and optional remote control of a BELABOX or Moblin. It reaches your OBS through a browser source that you add to OBS yourself.

It is an automation aid, not a guarantee. Networks, servers, OBS and your encoder can all fail, and so can we. A scene switch can come late or not at all, so keep an eye on your stream and keep a way to switch scenes or stop it by hand. Settings you change on a BELABOX or a phone through NOALBS Cloud (WiFi, modems, the hotspot) can cut it off from us, and then we can’t undo them: keep a way to reach your device without us.
