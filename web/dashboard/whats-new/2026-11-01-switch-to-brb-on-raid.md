If you use **Stop stream after a raid**, NOALBS can now switch to your BRB scene as the stream stops, so the stream
ends on BRB instead of whatever was on air when you raided out.

To turn it on, open a switcher, go to **Switching** and turn on **Switch to BRB after a raid** under
**Start, end & raids**. It needs **Stop stream after a raid** on and the chat bot connected, and works on Twitch and
Kick.

It uses the BRB scene of the stream server you're streaming through if that server has its own, and otherwise the
switcher's BRB scene from **Scenes**.
