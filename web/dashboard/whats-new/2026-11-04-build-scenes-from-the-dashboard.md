The **Sources** and **Scenes** cards on the **Live** page can now change OBS itself, not just show and hide what's
there. Tap **Edit** on either card to start. Tapping a source or scene outside Edit mode still only shows, hides or
switches it, so nothing gets moved or removed by accident on a phone.

In the **Sources** card's Edit mode you can:

- **Add a source:** a media source (SRT, RTMP, RIST and other stream URLs), a VLC video source, a browser source or
  a text source. You can also add a source or scene you already have in OBS to another scene.
- **Edit** media, VLC, browser and text sources: the stream URL, the page, the text and its font, and their other
  settings. URLs show hidden, since they often hold a key.
- **Position** any source: drag it and pull its corners on a picture of your screen, use a preset like "fit the
  screen" or "a quarter of the screen, bottom right", or type the exact size. The OBS preview shows behind it when
  you're allowed to see the preview.
- **Reorder** sources by dragging the handle on the left, like your stream servers.
- **Duplicate** a source with its settings, filters and position, or **remove** it from a scene.

In the **Scenes** card's Edit mode you can add a new scene, or remove one. The scene on air and the scenes NOALBS
switches to (LIVE, LOW, BRB and the others) can't be removed until you pick other ones.

Admins and owners can add, edit, duplicate and remove. Operators can reorder and position sources. Adding, editing,
positioning and removing show in the activity feed.

A few things stay in OBS: picking files from your PC (sources here play stream URLs, and ones that already play a
file keep it), adding to or removing from groups, and the order of your scenes.
