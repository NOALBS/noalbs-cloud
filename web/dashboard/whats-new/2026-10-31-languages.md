The dashboard and the chat bot are now available in Dansk, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, 日本語, 한국어,
Norsk bokmål, Nederlands, Polski, Português (Brasil), Русский, Svenska, Türkçe, Українська, 简体中文 and 繁體中文,
as well as English.

- **Dashboard:** open the account menu at the bottom left and pick one under **Language**. **Auto** follows your
  browser's language.
- **Chat bot commands:** the bot now replies to commands in your language too. Open a switcher, go to
  **Chat & commands** and pick a **Bot language**. The commands you type stay the same; only the bot's replies
  change. They're whole sentences, and numbers are written the way your language writes them. They build on the
  translations from the original NOALBS, whose translators are credited on the [licenses page](/licenses).

## Translated with AI

The translations were made with AI help and most haven't been checked by a native speaker yet, so some of them may
be wrong or read oddly. If you spot one, let us know by
[opening an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/NOALBS/noalbs-cloud/issues) or on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/efWu5HWM2u). You can also fix it yourself: the translations are plain `.po` files, and
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/NOALBS/noalbs-cloud/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#translations) explains how.
