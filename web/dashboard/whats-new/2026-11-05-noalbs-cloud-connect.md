**NOALBS Cloud Connect** is a new desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. It connects OBS to your switcher like
the browser source does, and because it runs on your PC, it can do more:

- **Start, stop and restart OBS** from the dashboard. The Live page offers **Start OBS** when OBS isn't running or
  has crashed.
- **The OBS watchdog** restarts OBS after a crash or when it stops responding, and can go live again for you.
- **OBS logs and crash reports** upload to the dashboard, so you can see what went wrong without asking for the
  file. Stream keys and IP addresses are hidden unless you choose to show them.
- **Programs** like Streamer.bot can start and stop together with OBS, or from the dashboard.
- **Several OBS setups** on one computer, each paired to its own switcher.
- **Updates** are checked automatically and installed when you click **Install and restart**.

The browser source isn't going anywhere: Connect is just another way to connect, so use whichever you like.

To try it, open your switcher's **Settings** page and find the **NOALBS Cloud Connect** card. Download the app for
your computer, add a setup and pair it. The [Connect guide](https://docs.noalbs.com/guides/connect/) walks
through it step by step.

The switcher's owner decides who can start, stop and restart OBS, see its logs, and manage programs. Programs
are owners-only by default. Keep these tight: they let people run software on your PC.

## It's brand new

This is the first release, so expect some rough edges. The app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS warn
you the first time you open it; the guide shows how to get past that. If something doesn't work the way you
expect, please tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/efWu5HWM2u). It helps a lot.
