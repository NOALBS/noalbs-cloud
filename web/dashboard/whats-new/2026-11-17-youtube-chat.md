NOALBS now works with YouTube, next to Twitch and Kick. Sign in with your YouTube channel, or keep signing in the way
you do now and just link the channel to a switcher.

To link it, open a switcher, go to **Chat & commands** and tap **Connect YouTube channel**. Sign in with the Google
account the channel belongs to (pick the brand account if your channel uses one). The same commands, aliases and
permissions you set up for Twitch and Kick work in your YouTube chat.

- **While you're live:** YouTube only has a live chat during a broadcast, so the bot looks for yours once OBS starts
  streaming. It usually finds it within a minute of going live.
- **Who answers:** the shared NOALBS bot replies in your chat. To reply from a YouTube channel of your own instead,
  tap **Use your own bot** under **Bot**.
- **Ignoring chatters:** `!noalbs ignore @handle` works with YouTube handles.

You can take NOALBS's access away at any time by removing the channel on the **Chat & commands** page or at
[Google's third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
