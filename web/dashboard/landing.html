Automatic scene switching
for IRL streams.

NOALBS Cloud watches your stream server every second and puts OBS on LOW or BRB when the signal goes, then back to LIVE when it returns. Everything else is on your phone: scenes, sources, audio, your BELABOX or Moblin, and your chat.

Set up in ten minutes. Then stop thinking about it.

The switching rules are NOALBS’s own, the ones IRL streamers have run on their own PCs for years.

  1. 1

    Add one browser source

    Paste your link into OBS. It talks to OBS on your PC and connects out to us, so there are no ports to open and nothing else to run.

  2. 2

    Add your stream server

    BELABOX Cloud, SRT Live Server, MediaMTX, NGINX and eight more. Test connection tells you right away if the stats URL works.

  3. 3

    Pick scenes and triggers

    Your LIVE, LOW and BRB scenes, bitrate and RTT triggers, backup servers by priority, retry attempts. Then go live and walk.

Questions

Do I need to install anything? +

No. You add a browser source to OBS and turn on OBS’s WebSocket server (built into OBS 28 and later). The browser source talks to OBS on your PC and connects out to NOALBS Cloud, so there’s nothing to forward on your router.

What happens if NOALBS Cloud goes down mid-stream? +

Your stream keeps going. OBS stays on whatever scene it was on and you can switch by hand as always. The browser source reconnects by itself, and switching picks up again.

Is my OBS password safe? +

It’s stored encrypted and only sent to your own browser source. The browser-source link is shown once when you make it; we keep only a hash of it. Stream server passwords and API keys are write-only, so the dashboard never shows them again.

Which stream server should I use? +

The one your encoder already sends to. NOALBS reads its stats page, so a BELABOX Cloud relay, an SRT Live Server or MediaMTX on a VPS, or a hosted IRL server all work. Add a second one as a backup and NOALBS uses whichever has a feed, by priority.

Can my mods help without getting my passwords? +

Yes. Operators can switch scenes and run the stream without seeing your stats URLs or keys. Admins can also change settings and invite people. In chat you decide which commands moderators, VIPs, subscribers or everyone may use.

What does it cost? +

The free plan covers one switcher with switching, remote control, chat commands and BELABOX and Moblin control. Pro adds more switchers, stream servers and team members, longer history and your own chat bot. You can see the plans after signing in.
